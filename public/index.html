📖 How to Play

  1. Create a game and share the Room Code with a friend
  2. Buy champions from the shop using gold
  3. Place them from bench onto your board
  4. Match traits (e.g. 2 Warriors) for bonus stats
  5. Battle happens automatically every 30 seconds
  6. Win by reducing your opponent's HP to 0!
